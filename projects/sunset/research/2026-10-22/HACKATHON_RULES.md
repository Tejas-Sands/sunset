## The relevant wording

The rendered submission section says: “Submissions open 1 Nov 2026 · 00:00 UTC”. Elsewhere the homepage instructs: “Build your project during the hackathon”. Its FAQ describes the event starting November 1 followed by two weeks of development. These statements should be read together. [Homepage](https://nearhackathon.com/).

HACKATHON.md says: “Submissions are accepted only during the development phase”. It gives the November 1–14 API window and describes a 403 outside it. That is a published submission restriction; this research did not send a submission to test enforcement. [Agent instructions](https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md).

## What remains unresolved

Neither reviewed page explicitly specifies an allowed pre-existing-code policy, a required first-commit date, a permitted percentage of earlier work, or a rule stating that all pre-November code is automatically disqualified. Equally, the submission opening does not constitute permission to complete the project beforehand. Only an explicit organizer policy or clarification can settle that eligibility question.

The accurate correction to the earlier chat is therefore: waiting to code was a conservative planning decision, not a verified disqualification rule. Preserve that distinction in future discussion. The current plan retains its coding window for eligibility caution, while this research remains preparation; the official pages do not expressly certify every category of preparation either.
