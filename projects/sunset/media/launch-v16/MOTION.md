## What changes from V15

V15 spent 4.1 seconds on the unchanged refund card, 5.4 seconds on the mainly white reader passage, repeated the circle/point/sentence construction for motivation and custody, and held the final card for about 3.7 seconds. The new script keeps the maker's purpose but removes that succession of administrative reading stops.

Collage passages follow **travel → reveal → brief landing → departure**. Plan distinct discoveries about every 0.6–1.2 seconds: wide into macro, foreground crossing the lens, camera travelling into a paper aperture, or a new material world. That is a directing target, not a frame-difference score or creative approval. A whole-frame zoom or random jiggle alone does not satisfy it.

Keep a main phrase readable across several changing compositions. Foreground, middle and background have separate trajectories. Essential letters stay clear at the reading landing; texture can move around them. Use extreme crops and occasional abrupt scale changes when they reveal something. Avoid completing a neat ticket grid and then waiting.

There is one minimal payoff at 23–26 seconds. Funding and release stay inside the travelling material world; the white circle does not restart the explanation. The real reader action is 2.7 seconds at actual elapsed speed. The logo is reached through a transformation and has at most a 1.5-second final hold.
