# SUNSET v11 — tactile 3D launch film

The operator explicitly requested real 3D objects that feel spongy, kinetic and dynamic, with no intersections or objects passing through one another. This supersedes the earlier 2D preference. The latest handbook remains the useful product example. The operator is asking for a launch film with product moments, not another long instructional presentation.

## Selected production approach

Use the available cached Three.js0.180 native WebGL2 pipeline. Blender is not installed. Reuse capture transport and licensed inputs, not older metallic scenes or old movies. New actual rounded meshes use cream/coral/mint satin materials, studio lighting and a grounded surface. Labels belong on UV material or protected screen typography; loose label planes must not cut objects.

Sponginess comes from visible contact-driven squash, lateral bulge and damped recovery. Preserve volume approximately/exactly according to the chosen affine deformation. This is authored analytic deformation with contact constraints, not a claim of a full soft-body solver. Contact must visibly cause the change; perpetual idle wobble does not establish softness.

Movement must feel like a launch: changing scale, purposeful camera travel, momentum, compressed/rebounding contacts and a larger access/release payoff. Keep a small cast of recognisable objects and coherent spatial relationships. Whole short phrases remain legible. The handbook is visible in a brief actual UI moment rather than most of the film being document text.

## Collision and visibility contract

- Check actual post-deformation geometry bounds, floor support and pair separation. A model-space box or original undeformed radius is insufficient.
- Use deterministic dense contact substeps with a between-step displacement bound. A positive gap at only30fps does not rule out tunnelling.
- Keep centres/lanes ordered during contact choreography, reserve space for squash bulges, and resolve bounds before render.
- Expose actual live solid meshes, transforms and camera to independent inspection. CPU geometry and reported bounds must match what is rendered; shader-only deformation needs equivalent verified support bounds.
- Camera near-plane clearance, essential text occlusion and actual 3D interpenetration are separate checks. Normal front/behind occlusion is allowed; passing through solids is not.
- Designed components of one continuous object must be identified. Do not silently exclude unrelated colliding objects or hide a failure with a camera crop.

## Staged output

First produce a few native material/contact poses, a twelve-frame throughput probe, then an eight-second native1080p/30fps motion proof. Root and a fresh reviewer must assess tactile contact, pace, camera and spatial coherence before extending the edit. Dense strips/proof metrics support the review; they do not establish that it feels good.

Target full cut: roughly30–36seconds with a short real handbook glimpse and few clear messages. Exact shot duration is an implementation call after the feel test. Preserve the product problem, NEAR custody/timely committed release and useful retained-copy payoff without turning them into architecture slides. Financial actions remain illustrations; the new handbook has no separately established onchain funded release. No app/contract/signing/deployment work is needed.

Retain the original SUNSET mark and warm personality. Background music/effects only, no generated narration. No on-screen product URL or music-credit block; links, feedback and required attribution stay in accompanying text. Keep generated media/caches/fonts/dependencies private and ignored. Preserve all older movies.
