## Material, type and composition

The palette changes by chapter: blue-black/cream, mustard/rust, yellow caution graphics, full-color graffiti, monochrome disc, warm tabletop, muted cutouts, red-black comic, grayscale iris. This supplies scene identity without a permanent template.

Type has several physical identities: oversized oblique uppercase sans; individual ransom-style letter slips with inconsistent widths/colors; bold black sentence fragments on white paper strips; handwritten comic balloons; red marker lettering; and an ornate white closing script. Tiny credits, HUD markings and bottom karaoke captions form a secondary texture/reading layer. Main words are deliberately cropped and overlapped. Legibility arrives in bursts as composition settles; the captions provide continuity underneath.

Print grain, halftone dots, rough graffiti, glossy device edges, photographic wood, flat cartoons and artificial scratches coexist. The checkerboard is visibly part of the composited background, not evidence that the MP4 contains transparency. Overlap establishes foreground/background: arrows cover letter slips, figures cover posters, paper labels cross characters, comic gutters divide moving panels.
