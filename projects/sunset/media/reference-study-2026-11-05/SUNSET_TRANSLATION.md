## Why V12 misses that grammar

Observation: V12's nine decoded beat samples keep the same cream floor, padded workshop-kit publication, coral seal and mint pieces. Most image changes come from camera/object orientation and the later page opening. “Free to use. Creators paid.” remains on the same lower caption plate throughout. The seal hides some of the title in the opening views. The visible checklist is an illustrative workshop page.

Inference: this reads primarily as a material/object demonstration. Its repeated set and camera path spend attention on the publication's volume while adding little to the idea of public value or the human maker. The line states the result but the scene never establishes the creator's work as the reason for compensation. Increasing orbit speed or adding a new texture would preserve that structural problem.

Recommendation: keep SUNSET's cream, terracotta, deep green, mint, original mark and occasional tangible depth. Change the construction of the film: large type sets the thought; three distinct useful works establish breadth; a paper-to-person reveal makes the maker visible; a clean final composition holds the promise. Depth should serve one reveal, not determine the whole cut.

## One treatment: Open work, visible maker

Original editorial paper collage with a precise mask handoff and a calm final poster. Approximately 8.0 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps delivery. Keep a small, persistent **Concept preview** label. The three complete phrases are the only essential reading. Secondary example labels are optional recognition aids.

These timings are a proposal. A proof must check the actual rendered reading time, especially the six-word opening; if it is rushed at phone size, lengthen the proof modestly instead of scattering or accelerating words. The initial three examples can change beneath a fixed phrase, so compositional energy does not shorten its readable hold.

The compensation message is a product intention inside a labelled concept. This proof does not teach the full mechanism. A later main film still needs the distinction between timely release, claimable creator credit, a separate successful withdrawal, and conditional sponsor refunds. Do not attach a Paid state to a page reveal or imply that a reader pays.

## Executable assets and construction

Use the existing cached, licensed SUNSET typeface files and original mark. The original homepage illustration can supply compatible people/art fragments only after checking that its crop works at delivery size; otherwise draw a small original adult maker/desk cutout in SVG or native 2D shapes. The subject should read as a person doing work, not a mascot or faceless token.

New original assets needed:

- Three simple, legible resource faces: an explanatory lesson diagram, workshop preparation sheet and practical repair guide. Their invented content remains illustrative. Use everyday subjects, without terminal commands or Git material.
- One adult creator cutout with separate hand, pencil, draft-sheet and desk layers; up to three simple adult supporter cutouts if the composition benefits from them.
- Paper-edge masks, a printed rule, two or three quiet paper/ink textures generated from original procedural patterns, and an orderly copy fan derived from the original resource art.

Build as deterministic native 2D layers: text, vector/raster cutouts, alpha masks, affine transforms, shallow independent parallax and a single short perspective paper tilt. Existing rendering tools can composite the 240 delivery frames. No new full 3D scene, long physically simulated sequence or dependency is required for the proof. Establish one representative still for each of the poster, collage, maker reveal and final promise before rendering the complete interval.

Sound is separate from this visual proposal. The reference audio has not been listened to here. For the first proof, silence is acceptable; a later original short paper/contact sound design can mark the three semantic handoffs. Do not reuse reference music or generate narration by default.

## Originality and review

Take the editing principles, not the assets or exact scene order. No reference pixels, audio, Gorillaz characters, portraits, magazine branding, phone branding, tracing or copied compositions enter the SUNSET proof. Avoid reproducing the second reference's ocean → trees → hills → AI abbreviation sequence. Our connecting device is a useful page's printed rule/edge, with the creator as the semantic reveal.

Before calling the proof successful, inspect its actual decoded frames at native and phone sizes. Confirm the three phrases have stable readable holds; the maker and three resource categories are recognisable; every composition change adds meaning; the brief depth tilt never hides the important copy; and the final promise receives a real hold. Codec, contrast and geometry checks support that review but cannot establish that the creative direction works for the operator. Preserve V12 and earlier artifacts unchanged.
