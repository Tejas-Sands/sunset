# SUNSET v5 — considered storylines

The operator approved v4's visual style and requested clearer message, adult tone, legibility and image quality. These are different narrative paths, not alternative palettes. The coordinator selected the first for the measured 24-second cut; the narration is locked in [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md).

## Chosen — The resource outlasts the website

- Device: one learning pack remains the visual carrier across its host, community funding, release and retained use.
- Form: physical-object camera chain with controlled glyph/crop punctuation and short actual UI proof.
- First two seconds: the website-shaped outer layer recedes; the solid resource remains. The line is “Useful learning should outlast a website.”
- Beats: premise -> community funding on NEAR -> matching-key release and creator withdrawal -> missed-deadline refund path -> retained offline copy -> original mark and address.
- Look: graphite and neutral ivory, restrained copper material, protected high-contrast copy.
- Sound: measured adult male narration over a restrained pulse; a clear release turn; quiet confidence on the retained copy.
- Why selected: it explains what the public-good outcome is before asking the viewer to understand cryptographic mechanics.

## Alternative — The creator's funding agreement

- Device: a creator's finished pack and a clear capped funding agreement become the whole world.
- Form: agreement details made physical, then a narrow real payment-receipt proof and a public-resource result.
- First two seconds: the finished pack sits beside “PAY THE CREATOR / KEEP THE KNOWLEDGE.”
- Beats: finished work -> fixed cap/deadline -> release -> creator withdrawal -> public reuse.
- Look: neutral contract-paper surfaces and dark metal, with one paid-state accent.
- Sound: sober, precise, almost documentary.
- Why not selected: it gives money and protocol detail more time and risks making the film feel like a transaction walkthrough.

## Alternative — After the connection ends

- Device: open on the actual retained offline result, then reveal how the public resource was funded.
- Form: result-first rewind and object match cuts back to the retained copy.
- First two seconds: an offline resource remains usable as the host disappears; “KEEP THE COPY.”
- Beats: retained result -> rewind to community escrow -> on-time key and payout -> return to public copy -> CTA.
- Look: mostly neutral ivory, a strong graphite funding world and a restrained copper reveal.
- Sound: near-silence at the first result, then a grounded pulse through the reveal.
- Why not selected: opening directly on the current sample game overweights the learner-facing quiz and delays the adult sponsor/creator offer.

## What every option leaves out

A separate NFT lesson, a five-question walkthrough, pretend wallet signing, adoption numbers, investment language and claims of permanent hosting. Token maintenance rights remain available in the product evidence and can receive a separate short film later.
