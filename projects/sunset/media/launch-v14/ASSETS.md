## portrait

Project file: `.publication/sunset-launch-v14/assets/creator-portrait.png`. Exact dimensions/hash/alpha are retained in `assets/provenance.json`.

Exact prompt:

> Generate an ORIGINAL high-resolution 2-by-2 editorial portrait atlas for a kinetic mixed-media film about people creating useful knowledge. Four distinct anonymous adult creators, each head-and-shoulders portrait in its own quadrant, with generous fully transparent gutters separating them. Original characters only: upper-left a woman with short dark curly hair and round glasses in a terracotta work shirt, facing right; upper-right a dark-skinned man with closely cropped hair in an ochre overshirt, three-quarter view; lower-left a woman with long dark wavy hair and a mint studio jacket, facing left; lower-right an older adult with silver hair and a navy textured shirt, near-profile. Sophisticated art direction: tactile painted-paper and gouache portrait sculpture, strong ink contour, realistic adult facial proportions, carefully lit layered depth, subtle halftone/photocopy grain and paper edge, warm skin tones, ink/cream/terracotta/mint/ochre accents. Distinctive clear silhouettes, expressive but focused people, premium music-video editorial cutout artwork. Each quadrant must have one complete bust without touching other quadrants; no frames, layout grid lines, text, logos, watermarks, tools or extra hands. Avoid cute/chibi proportions, generic stock-photo smiles, existing animated characters, green-haired musicians, hats or recognizable celebrity likenesses. Real transparent alpha behind and between the four cutouts. This is original illustrative asset art, not real SUNSET users or testimonials. Render the largest crisp resolution possible, ideally square.

Project file: `.publication/sunset-launch-v14/assets/workroom.png`. Exact dimensions/hash/alpha are retained in `assets/provenance.json`.

Exact prompt:

> Generate one ORIGINAL cinematic photographic studio/workroom background for a fast mixed-media editorial motion film. Wide landscape 16:9 composition. A raw creative workshop seen at eye level: richly textured dark blue-green painted concrete wall with abstract terracotta and mustard screen-print collage marks, layered off-white blank poster sheets and a metal poster rail on the wall; a wooden worktable with cutting mat, paper scraps, a few books and ink bottles in the lower foreground; an out-of-focus dark vertical shelf near the right foreground for depth and occlusion; warm practical lamp light from one side with realistic shadows. Keep the middle wall relatively open for placing separate portrait/poster cutouts during animation. The space should feel tactile, lived-in and visually rich, not a corporate office, SaaS mockup or empty minimalist room. No people, readable writing, letters, logos, brands, interfaces, warning text or watermarks. Original abstract markings only, no famous artwork. Sharp detailed wall/wood/paper textures and plausible optical depth; restrained cinematic contrast. This is illustrative scenery for a concept film, not a photograph of an actual SUNSET event.
