## Why v3 was still distant

V3 kept a regular left-heading/right-diagram stage for most of its eight equal sections. Its line geometry and flat-image camera scaling did not reveal solid surfaces, actual changing perspective, foreground occlusion or light response. A darker palette and more blockchain symbols could not solve that structural mismatch.

The stronger direction combines SaasWAVE's camera/object continuity with LILIUM's composition and typographic editing. It requires whole-frame changes of hierarchy, authored arrivals and held readable results, rather than uniform activity.

## Rendering research and limits

The installed Chromium was tested with an actual WebGL2 context and SwiftShader. The scene uses isolated pinned Three.js0.180.0 modules cached under ignored .publication, without changing application dependencies. [PerspectiveCamera](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/PerspectiveCamera.html) provides actual perspective; [MeshPhysicalMaterial](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/MeshPhysicalMaterial.html) provides physical material properties. Its documented per-pixel cost motivates limiting transmission-heavy surfaces and testing capture throughput before the full render. The [r180 source](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180) identifies the pinned implementation; retain its MIT license with cached dependencies.
