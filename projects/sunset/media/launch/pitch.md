# SUNSET launch story

**The sponsors need a wallet. The learners don’t.**

SUNSET makes a public learning resource fundable. Adults sponsor the release of an already-finished pack under a fixed agreement. A timely matching key creates the creator’s capped credit; the creator withdraws it. Missed funding or release deadlines let contributors claim and withdraw refunds. After successful release, learners can verify the pack, download the game and keep a copy without an account or wallet.

The maintenance token carries a concrete responsibility: its current holder chooses a verified discovery mirror for the same resource. Transferring that role preserves the content commitments and creator payment terms.

NEAR supplies the public agreement, native escrow, settlement and transferable maintenance right. The learner receives a file.

## The bigger idea

Useful resources take work to make. The people making them deserve a clear way to get paid. The people learning from them deserve something they can keep.

SUNSET brings those interests together around one finished resource and one bounded funding goal. Sponsors support the public release. The creator earns the agreed amount through the release rules. A learner can carry the downloaded game beyond the website and its internet connection.

Our prototype starts with an original learning adventure. We want to learn from teachers, libraries and sponsors about which finished resources would be worth releasing next.

**Support once. Share forever.**

## What the demonstration establishes

A 0.1-testNEAR campaign was approved, funded, released and withdrawn. The original creator received an actual native transfer and the withdrawal callback finalized. The maintenance NFT changed hands; the old holder’s mirror update failed, and the new holder’s mirror was accepted and verified. The browser-downloaded game completed all five questions in a fresh browser with offline emulation enabled before file loading.

This is a testnet prototype with operator-controlled demo accounts. It does not establish independent review, demand, permanent hosting, an external audit or mainnet readiness. Real expiry-refund demonstrations and maximum-state/failure-path protocol tests remain outstanding. Browser financial controls remain rehearsal; live signatures in this demonstration use the CLI.
