The sponsors need a wallet. The learners don't.

This is SUNSET.

A way to fund learning resources once, and let everyone keep a copy.

Adults sponsor the public release of a finished learning pack.

The agreement fixes the creator, the funding cap, the reviewer, and the deadlines.

Contributions go into a contract on NEAR testnet.

Its funding and release rules are publicly inspectable.

A matching release key, published on time, credits the agreed creator.

The creator then withdraws that credit.

Miss the funding or release deadline, and contributors can claim and withdraw refunds.

After a successful release, download the game and keep it.

Close the website. Turn off the internet. Keep playing.

No learner account. No learner wallet.

And the token has a job.

Its current holder can maintain a verified discovery mirror.

Transfer it, and pass on maintenance responsibility.

The content stays public. The creator's payment stays fixed.

SUNSET.

Support once. Share forever.
