# Original SUNSET artwork provenance

The final film reuses two original built-in imagegen illustrations through cropping, composition and transforms. Their fictional adult subjects are illustrative metaphors, not actual SUNSET users or testimonials. Reference videos informed motion direction; their pixels, audio, characters and branding are excluded. No new image generation occurred during final consolidation.

The nine exact used RGBA textures are frozen in `.publication/sunset-final/assets/`. [bundle.json](bundle.json) records file hashes, dimensions and pixel hashes. Only `creator.png` and `hands.png` derive from generated imagery. Workshop cover/agenda, inside cover, clasp, key, archive and SUNSET mark are native project graphic artwork. Original source filenames below are historical identities; these files are not runtime dependencies.

## Maker portrait

Original source `creator-portrait.png`, 1254×1254 RGBA, SHA-256 `a68f23e7b9578f569cfdbaeb5bd2cef4efa31e8d3052f2ae4815ab5710d08d47`. The accepted render crops the top-left quadrant, fits it to 570×690, and places it at (25,25) on a 620×745 ochre card. The final `creator.png` freezes those composed pixels exactly.

Exact original generation prompt:

> Generate an ORIGINAL high-resolution 2-by-2 editorial portrait atlas for a kinetic mixed-media film about people creating useful knowledge. Four distinct anonymous adult creators, each head-and-shoulders portrait in its own quadrant, with generous fully transparent gutters separating them. Original characters only: upper-left a woman with short dark curly hair and round glasses in a terracotta work shirt, facing right; upper-right a dark-skinned man with closely cropped hair in an ochre overshirt, three-quarter view; lower-left a woman with long dark wavy hair and a mint studio jacket, facing left; lower-right an older adult with silver hair and a navy textured shirt, near-profile. Sophisticated art direction: tactile painted-paper and gouache portrait sculpture, strong ink contour, realistic adult facial proportions, carefully lit layered depth, subtle halftone/photocopy grain and paper edge, warm skin tones, ink/cream/terracotta/mint/ochre accents. Distinctive clear silhouettes, expressive but focused people, premium music-video editorial cutout artwork. Each quadrant must have one complete bust without touching other quadrants; no frames, layout grid lines, text, logos, watermarks, tools or extra hands. Avoid cute/chibi proportions, generic stock-photo smiles, existing animated characters, green-haired musicians, hats or recognizable celebrity likenesses. Real transparent alpha behind and between the four cutouts. This is original illustrative asset art, not real SUNSET users or testimonials. Render the largest crisp resolution possible, ideally square.

## Creator hands

Original source `creator-cutout.png`, 1536×1024 RGBA, SHA-256 `d88f268d9044448951d19b8bcc08018e906d1f7a7ae3b1e15675d7371926522f`. Its alpha extrema are 0–254. The final `hands.png` preserves its RGBA pixels; on-screen scale/rotation/projection are authored animation, not observed hand movement.

Exact original generation prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Generate ONE original transparent-background editorial collage cutout asset for an animated SUNSET public-knowledge project film. Subject: an adult human creator at work, shown as a cropped over-the-shoulder torso and two anatomically correct hands, one holding a pencil and annotating a lesson manuscript on a small desk; no face is needed. Make the tangible act of creating useful knowledge unmistakable. Horizontal composition with the human working gesture concentrated centrally and a strong clean silhouette. Art direction: sophisticated mixed-media editorial collage: monochrome photographic realism in the hands and fabric, xerox/photocopy grain, subtle halftone ink, a narrow irregular cream paper-cut border, a small terracotta accent on the pencil and one mint paper tab. Rich authentic paper and clothing texture, crisp shape separation, adult design-magazine quality, restrained directional shadow within the object. The whole scene including the cropped desk/paper should form ONE isolated cutout on real transparent alpha, leaving generous transparency around its perimeter. No rectangular white canvas or baked checkerboard. The lesson paper may have abstract grey ink lines and a simple geometric diagram, but NO readable words, logos, brand marks, terminal/code/UI, celebrity likeness, existing characters, ransom typography, extra fingers, floating unrelated objects, watermarks or captions. Do not imitate any named artist. This is an illustrative metaphor for a creator's work, not a photograph of a real SUNSET customer. Deliver the original visual asset only; final animated typography will be composed separately.

These prompts requested individual still assets. The animation does not establish a real workshop, customer adoption or additional protocol execution. Original image source identities and prompts survive independently of discarded production studies.
