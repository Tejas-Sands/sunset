# What to add for a serious SUNSET launch

Creative recommendations following the operator's9 November request. These additions are proposed, not silently added to the 31s motion revision. Keep the ending as the original logo/tagline; demo and repository links can stay in the accompanying post.

## Priorities

1. **Natural narration that carries the why.** Give the human value a voice: “Useful work can be free without leaving its creator unpaid.” Keep the approved visible words and let the voice explain the connection between the creator, sponsors and public access. The operator rejected dull robotic narration earlier; no voice is generated for V21. Record or choose the voice before timing and mixing the final voice pass.
2. **One concrete creator and resource.** Follow one useful thing from preparation to release to use. A community workshop kit would be an understandable proposed example: a person makes it, a community supports its release, another organiser keeps it and uses it. The kit would need to be built/tested before being shown as a working SUNSET example. Existing handbook and Lantern Trail examples remain separately identified. One coherent example can make the emotional payoff more concrete than a gallery of unrelated resources.
3. **A short, factual proof beat.** The [recorded main testnet showcase](../../evidence/testnet-showcase.json) establishes an actual 0.1 testNEAR creator withdrawal and successful callback. The [browser evidence](../../evidence/browser-showcase.json) establishes actual downloaded-pack use with browser offline emulation enabled before file load. A restrained “Testnet payout verified / Offline reader verified” treatment can link the promise to demonstrable behaviour. The native testnet resource was Lantern Trail, not the later handbook or a proposed workshop kit. Those records do not establish independent customers or livelihood outcomes.
4. **One clear refund branch.** “If agreed funding or release conditions are missed, sponsors can claim refunds.” The [actual expiry/refund receipts](../../evidence/testnet-refunds.json) support both paths and native principal withdrawals. Show a claimable refund before a separate withdrawal; avoid implying automatic return. Give this explanation its own short beat if added, preserving time for the success path and reader benefit.
5. **A sound pass built around the chosen voice.** Place pauses at the maker reveal, key acceptance and paired payoff; lower the music under narration and retain contact sounds on visible actions. V21 already has recorded Foley; levels and source provenance are checked, while the final voice balance and perceptual listening remain future work.

My recommendation is to do **narration plus one coherent use case first**, then add the smallest factual proof/refund beats the resulting timing can comfortably hold. Animation can carry the transitions; the story should establish who benefits, what sponsors make possible, what NEAR enforces and what the audience can actually do.
