Useful work can be free to use while its maker gets paid.

SUNSET lets a community sponsor a prepared public release, support its creator, and keep a copy without an account or wallet.

Testnet demo: https://sunset-lemon.vercel.app
Source: https://github.com/Tejas-Sands/sunset
