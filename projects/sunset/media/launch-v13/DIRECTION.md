Eight-second silent concept, proposed after studying every native frame of the two exact supplied reference files. This proof addresses visual style and the creator-facing purpose, rather than replacing the full proposed [main script](../launch-v12/SCRIPT_DRAFT.md).

An opening workshop-paper macro pulls back into three original paper examples, establishing that useful work can be shared freely. A held coral print divider expands into a mask revealing the maker behind that work. The maker's circular accent becomes an aperture carrying the whole word **FREE**, then expands into the clean SUNSET promise. The short opening phrase, **Useful work. Shared freely.**, compresses the longer why for this eight-second visual test. **Help creators keep creating.** supplies the human reason. **Free to use. Creators paid.** receives the final stable hold.

The visual language combines independent cutout layers and tangible print with restrained masks and fixed type. It changes composition rather than extending the earlier padded publication's camera path. Paper artwork is native 2D; the original generated creator illustration preserves its alpha. Two-dimensional occlusion is intentional compositing, not evidence of solid-body collision checking. The film is a labelled concept with original example faces, not a newly implemented resource or actual creator, user, funding, payment or browser recording.

Source: `render-proof.py`. Uses the installed Pillow/FFmpeg and cached licensed Bricolage Grotesque/Geist fonts, with original SUNSET mark geometry from `public/icon.svg`. No package installation or reference pixels/audio. Asset provenance is recorded separately in this directory.

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 projects/sunset/media/launch-v13/render-proof.py --poses
python3 projects/sunset/media/launch-v13/render-proof.py --motion-poses
python3 projects/sunset/media/launch-v13/render-proof.py --render
```

The `--render` command runs only after review of the actual native preview poses. It writes 240 native 1920×1080 PNG frames, performs one silent CRF14 H.264/limited-range BT.709/faststart encode, fully decodes the result, and writes reading-window/bounds/contrast/source-hash records plus decoded beat/cut/100ms mask sheets under ignored `.publication/sunset-launch-v13/`. It refuses to overwrite an existing encoded proof. Authored text records support visual review and do not establish creative approval. Older movies and sources are untouched.
