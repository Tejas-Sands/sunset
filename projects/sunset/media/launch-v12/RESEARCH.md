# SUNSET story reset — 5 November 2026

Status: researched proposal, not an approved new full film or a new funded resource. The operator rejected V11's Git-command example because it suggests that using SUNSET requires terminal commands. Technical render checks did not test that misunderstanding.

## The user's central why

People freely share valuable lessons, workshop material and useful guides. The people doing that work deserve compensation and the ability to keep creating. SUNSET's mission is to keep valuable work accessible while a community supports the person who made it. A community workshop kit is one understandable example of this wider purpose, not the whole product.

The present mechanism compensates a prepared resource's public release under agreed conditions. It does not establish ongoing wages or guaranteed financial survival. The film should express the purpose positively: **Free to use. Creators paid.** Then show how that compensation becomes claimable, and why readers can keep free copies.

## Distinct value and limits

[Unglue.it's current FAQ](https://unglue.it/faq/) identifies its active program as Thanks-for-Ungluing: supporters reward already-free Creative Commons ebooks. It supports durable distribution and HTML files. Historical pledge/Buy-to-Unglue programs must not be described as currently active. [Kiwix](https://get.kiwix.org/en/) already provides offline knowledge tools. A PDF also works offline. Neither retained files nor supporting freely shared resources is a SUNSET invention.

The narrower proposition is **pooled, capped sponsorship with explicit onchain release conditions**. NEAR holds accepted principal; timely publication of the committed key creates creator credit, followed by a separate withdrawal. Missed funding/release conditions allow sponsors to claim principal and separately withdraw. [Kickstarter's current refund guidance](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236553-does-kickstarter-issue-refunds), dated6 August2026, instead describes funds going to creators and collected-pledge refund requests going to them. This supports a narrow custody comparison, not a guarantee of content quality or permanent hosting.

Ordinary publishing/donations may be preferable when an author is already funded, the edition changes continually, supporters only use conventional payments, or no one values conditional release enough to justify wallet friction. Sponsor willingness and independent user validation remain open.

## Constraints the new script must respect

- The exact reviewed/encrypted pack exists before campaign approval. Say **sponsor this edition's public release**, not pay upfront to write unfinished content.
- Current guides are original/properly authorised CC0 plain text and checklists. P2PU's CC BY-SA template and ordinary OpenStax books cannot be copied and relabelled CC0. No rich course or arbitrary PDF upload is implemented.
- Readers use a browser. A downloaded HTML guide needs no terminal, wallet or account. Initial live recovery/download needs available hosting/network.
- Browser financial controls are rehearsal/unsigned plans; live signatures happen separately. Onchain campaign creation is owner-only. Do not fake one-click live sponsorship or a self-service creator marketplace.
- Release creates credit, not an automatic wallet payment. Refunds are claims/withdrawals, not automatic or a satisfaction guarantee.
- NEAR does not certify usefulness, copyright or permanent hosting. Stewardship can select a mirror for the same committed bytes, not edit the edition or transfer copyright.
- Existing testnet receipts belong to their original resource. A future workshop example needs original content and genuine capture and cannot inherit those receipts.
