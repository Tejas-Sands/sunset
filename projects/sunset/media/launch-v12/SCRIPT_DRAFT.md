# Proposed main script — valuable work, shared freely

**Draft for operator review.** This replaces V11's developer-facing story. It does not implement a new resource or establish new onchain sponsorship. The accompanying short style sample is only a visual concept.

**The why:** people share incredible things for free. The people creating that value deserve support and the ability to keep creating. Lessons, workshop kits and useful guides can remain free to use while their creators are compensated.

**One sentence:** SUNSET lets a community sponsor valuable work's public release, compensate its creator under recorded release rules, and give everyone a useful copy to keep.

**Concrete example, not product definition:** one facilitator's prepared First Community Workshop Kit, several adult supporters and the next volunteer using it. Sponsorship compensates the kit's public release, not hosting a physical workshop. Brief lesson/guide examples establish the broader purpose without pretending every possible file format is implemented.

Captions-first: this works without generated narration. Hold whole phrases long enough to read; movement connects the actions rather than scattering letters. Music and practical contact/page sounds carry rhythm. A later human voice take needs its own script/mix against the locked edit.

The viewer should understand why creators deserve compensation, how sponsors enable public release, and who uses the free copy. The real result is a retained checklist in an offline browser guide. No terminal imagery or Git content appears. Reader access remains separate from sponsor wallet/signature requirements. The creator earns an agreed capped amount for this release; the story expresses support for a livelihood without promising guaranteed income.

The financial middle illustrates implemented testnet rules. Before a full movie calls the workshop passage real, that original guide must be authored and captured genuinely. It remains a local example unless a separately authorised campaign is completed. The project currently has curated testnet workflows, not a production self-service funding interface.
