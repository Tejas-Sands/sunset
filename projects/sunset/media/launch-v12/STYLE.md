# Proposed animation style

A short concept test after the research and draft script, not another finished launch movie. Preserve the original SUNSET mark, warm palette and the operator's soft3D preference. The user's central why supplies the preview line: **Free to use. Creators paid.** A workshop kit is one example among valuable lessons and guides.

**Look:** editorial typography with tangible designed publications. Cream studio floor, terracotta seal, mint supporter tickets, dark-green print. A prominent Workshop Kit title, page-edge graphics and coloured chapter tabs make the resource recognisable. Avoid terminal imagery, anonymous pillow blocks and unrelated coin clutter.

**Motion:** macro contact → moving three-quarter view → publication reveal. Compression causes lateral bulge, a quick rebound and damped settling. Reserve separate lanes with room for expanded silhouettes. Camera travel reveals relationships; essential phrases remain whole and legible.

**Small sample:** approximately5.5s using the existing proven six-solid choreography with redesigned nontechnical resource/ticket print, the why phrase and explicit Concept preview labelling. It previews weight/materials/camera only. Displayed guide content is illustrative, not new browser footage or a signed financial action. Silent, with no generated narration. Keep old movies/sources unchanged.

The proposed35s film alternates tactile3D with one brief real browser download/check/saved-result moment. A usable public copy is the payoff. The draft script must be reviewed before a new full film is produced.
