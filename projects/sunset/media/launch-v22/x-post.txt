Useful work can be free to use while its maker gets paid.

SUNSET lets a community sponsor a prepared public release. Meet the target and publish the matching key on time; the creator can claim payment. Missed conditions allow sponsor refunds.

The film follows an original workshop kit. Its testnet proof is the separate recorded Lantern Trail run, with all roles operated by one person.

Testnet demo: https://sunset-lemon.vercel.app
Source: https://github.com/Tejas-Sands/sunset
