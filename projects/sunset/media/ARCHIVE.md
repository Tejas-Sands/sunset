# Media history and cleanup

The accepted film now has one [source directory](launch/README.md) and one local ignored bundle, `.publication/sunset-final/`. Preserve that bundle separately: Git contains the renderer, prompts, licenses and hashes, not the generated film/audio/textures.

At the operator’s request, the superseded production folders, reference exports, duplicated movies and their virtual environments/models were deleted on 9 November 2026. [Cleanup evidence](../evidence/production-cleanup-2026-11-09.json) records the paths, byte counts and accepted-film identity. Genuine browser/offline evidence, public protocol evidence, original samples, private operator backups and Git history were retained.

Earlier tracked sources, scripts and reviews remain in the immutable [pre-cleanup media tree](https://github.com/Tejas-Sands/sunset/tree/6fcd6300403f1dd7424f234084c49baade16d40a/projects/sunset/media). The [previous launch guide](https://github.com/Tejas-Sands/sunset/blob/6fcd6300403f1dd7424f234084c49baade16d40a/projects/sunset/docs/LAUNCH_VIDEO.md) preserves the revision narrative. These links become remotely available after the operator pushes the local history.

Historical evidence JSON and build-ledger entries keep their original dates, measurements and scope. Their removed local artifact paths describe files that existed at the time; they are not current dependencies or promises to retain every superseded render. The final source and verifier work without those folders. Generated old movies are not recoverable from Git; their deletion was intentional.
