# Original sample content check

The Lantern Trail is an original five-question technical sample. This is a software-side arithmetic and wording review, not an independent educator review, curriculum alignment, classroom safety assessment or evidence of learning impact. Content schema validation cannot establish those outcomes.

| Puzzle | Independent arithmetic | Answer in the pack | Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest gate | 7 + 5 = 12 | 12 | Correct |
| Wooden bridge | 16 − 7 = 9 | 9 | Correct |
| Orchard path | 4 × 3 = 12 | 12 | Correct |
| River stones | 24 ÷ 6 = 4 | 4 | Correct |
| Coastal beacon | 3 × 8 − 5 = 19 | 19 | Correct |

Each correct integer appears exactly once among distinct choices. Explanations match the arithmetic. The five scenes form a finite objective: bring light back to a lighthouse. On-screen feedback lets a learner continue/replay; the standalone export permits retrying a wrong answer. No learner name, wallet or server identity is needed.

Questions use English and assume reading comprehension, elementary integer arithmetic and access to a compatible browser/device. Ages 7–10 is a demonstration label rather than validated age suitability. Two other seeded campaign titles reuse this same sample; they are scenario fixtures, not three separately reviewed educational products. No claim of teacher approval is made.

For an actual pilot, have an independent educator check language, difficulty, cultural context, accessibility and any local curriculum requirements; obtain adult sponsor feedback on a resource they would genuinely fund. Preserve that feedback as evidence without uploading children’s personal data. Do not replace public learning access with token ownership: stewardship concerns maintenance, not permission to learn.
