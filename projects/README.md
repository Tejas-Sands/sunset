# Main project candidates

**SUNSET is selected for the build. A local prototype and compiled contract were built on 1 November; live settlement and browser verification remain outstanding.** Its [maintained project directory](sunset/README.md) contains the complete architecture for a free Vercel, Supabase and NEAR testnet prototype. Other retained candidates remain available as alternatives. Research provenance lives in [the research directory](../research/).

[Additional candidates under investigation](../research/alternatives-2026-10-22/README.md) are kept separately until their differentiation and feasibility justify promotion.

The [new ownership and collective action proposals](../research/frontier-ideas-2026-10-22/README.md) record the original SUNSET and MOLT research.
