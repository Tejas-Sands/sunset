Proceed with SUNSET, with track expansion tied to observable behavior. The event is the November 1–14, 2026 NEAR HACKATHON; the live rules explicitly allow testnet-only entries, multiple fitting tracks, and code since the October 19 announcement. They also make named integrations optional. These findings remove eligibility uncertainty in the October research and weaken the case for adding paid/mainnet infrastructure solely for a track. [Live instructions](https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md), [event](https://nearhackathon.com/).

The best core is collective financing of a useful educational resource, a fixed creator buyout, verifiable public release or refund, and an independent offline export. This is a strong Open Innovation proposal and a plausible Social Money product once shared budgets and finite access grants actually work. Add a complete order/invoice, acceptance, payout/refund and receipt-export journey for Real-World Money. These are three natural targets; no functioning implementation is established by the existing design documents alone.

The most coherent expansion is bounded group procurement for those same packs and services. Independently budgeted buyers can compare seller offers, coordinate demand and settle through the same contract. Contract-enforced caps and adversarial rejection demonstrations then support Security; actual purchasing supports Agent Economy; coordination between independent participants supports Swarms. The official Swarms example explicitly permits scripted strategies without LLM calls, so a useful deterministic demonstration is possible at zero inference cost. Disclose strategy agents accurately. [Official Swarms example](https://github.com/nearai/near-hackathon-prompt-examples/blob/main/tracks/05-swarms.md).

Treat Autonomous Business as a seventh stretch requiring a complete autonomous seller loop. Do not claim Games merely because the resource is a maths game, Privacy merely because bytes are encrypted, or DeFi merely because escrow holds funds. NFTs, memecoins and investment research would dilute this product. The goal should be six related, evidenced tracks if the agent layer works, with three reliable targets if it does not.

The economic mechanism has precedent, including buy-to-release models already acknowledged in the research. Position the contribution as enforceable capped funding, independent fulfillment, refund safety and offline usability, rather than invention of buying assets into the commons. Actual educator demand, rights ownership and useful lesson quality remain unvalidated. Neither chain settlement nor a teacher signature proves educational impact.

Go/no-go gates are therefore concrete: a real NEAR Sandbox/testnet lifecycle; no API authority over balances; independently usable release; recoverable failed-release funds; and a freshly opened offline export with networking disabled. Build these before agent orchestration. Track selection should follow the final demonstrated evidence. Publication, signed deployment and final submission require the human decisions specified by the live rules, and the one-shot submission must wait for the exact reviewed payload.
